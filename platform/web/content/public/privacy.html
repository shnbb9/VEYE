Privacy Policy

Draft — prototype content. This page is placeholder wording for demonstration only. The final Privacy Policy will be written and approved by legal counsel and, in the live product, published and versioned from the Veye admin portal (Content → Legal Documents).

This draft describes how Veye would collect, use and protect member information. Nothing on this page is final legal wording.

1. Information We Collect

Account details you provide (such as name and email address, and optional phone number or ZIP code), the assessment answers and tracker entries you choose to record, and basic technical information needed to operate the service.

2. How Information Is Used

To show you your own results, progress and history; to operate and improve the Veye program; and to contact you about your account when you ask us to. Veye does not sell member data.

3. Health Information

Assessment results, blood-marker entries, body-composition records, mood entries and food diaries are sensitive information. They are used only to present your own progress and guidance inside the product.

4. In This Prototype

This demonstration stores everything in your own browser (localStorage) and transmits nothing. No account, message, email or record leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it completely.

5. Sharing

The live product would share information only with service providers needed to run Veye, under contract, and never for advertising. No third-party sharing occurs in this prototype.

6. Your Choices

You may review, correct or delete your information. In this prototype, deleting your browser's stored data for this site removes everything.

7. Security

The live product would protect member data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and audit logging. These protections are part of the future production build.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@veye.co.
