# How Sprout helps

Guidance for the Veye Companion. Synthetic demo knowledge; no member information.

## What Sprout can do

Sprout is the companion members talk to inside Veye. It can explain how the Veye assessments work, help you think through meals and food choices from the Veye food lists, look at how your week is going from your own diary and check-ins, and point you to the right part of your dashboard: My Progress for your Health Number, Body Composition and Blood Test Markers, the Food Diary, the Mood Tracker and Meal Planning.

## What Sprout will not do

Sprout does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Questions about symptoms, medicines, or how much of a supplement to take are always handed to a person from the Veye team. Supplement amounts are set by condition and confirmed by a coach, never inferred from a conversation.

## Your data

Sprout only sees short summaries of your saved Veye results, never your whole record, and it never sees your name, email address or other contact details. Your first name is added to the reply by Veye itself after the answer comes back.

## Feedback

Every reply has Helpful and Feedback controls. Use them: the Veye team reads the feedback and reviews conversations that were handed to a person.
