## Using the Veye site

The Veye site is designed to be simple and easy to use. Veye uses an internal AI assistant to guide you at your pace, concentrate on the things that work best for you, and give you personalized tips and advice. For best results, after you sign in Veye suggests that you start by filling in the sections on the left in order. Then make your food choices  -  this will help the Veye Companion customize your meals. Finally, fill out the food diary as often as possible. The Veye Companion will assess your choices and let you know what meals are perfect for you, and what can be improved.

## Technical help

### I can't sign in to my account

Check that you are using the email address you signed up with, and that Caps Lock is off. If you are still unable to sign in, close the tab and open Veye again before trying once more.

### I forgot my password

Use the show/hide control in the password field to confirm what you have typed. Password resets will be available from the sign-in screen; until then, email us and we will help you back in.

### A page will not load or looks broken

Refresh the page first. If that does not help, clear your browser cache and reload. A stale cached file is the most common cause of a page rendering incorrectly.

### My progress does not seem to be saving

Make sure you press Save on the screen you are filling in, and that your browser is not in private/incognito mode, which discards stored progress when the window closes.

### Which browsers and devices does Veye support?

Veye works on current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on desktop, tablet and phone. If you are on an older browser, updating it usually resolves display problems.

### My issue is still not fixed

Email help@veye.co with a short description of what happened and which page you were on, and we will take it from there.

## Diet and nutrition terminology

### What is metabolism?

Your metabolism is how you process and convert energy, and is controlled by AMPK. Metabolism controls your weight, mental focus, physical performance, how you age, and more. By following the Veye program you can control AMPK, and therefore your metabolism.

### Is intermittent fasting good for you?

Although intermittent fasting has shown promise in reducing senescent, or "zombie" cells, you can achieve the same effect without starving by eating a low calorie diet with the proper ratio of macronutrients. The Veye program restricts calories without sacrifice.

### Why do I need to eat protein at every meal?

To activate glucagon and AMPK, two important components to health.

### How much protein do I need?

Most women need 77grams per day and men 121grams, although this varies by the individual. The Veye program can help you determine your exact protein needs.

### What is the key to living longer?

Calorie restriction is the only known way to increase longevity. The Veye program allows calorie restriction without hunger or deprivation.

### Is a vegan diet the best one?

The research is inconclusive, although Veye respects individuals who choose this lifestyle. It is hard to get adequate protein on a vegan diet. Veye can help you maintain a vegan diet, or make healthy choices whatever your dietary preference is.

### Can you eat too much protein?

Yes, it is important to have the right protein prescription for you and your lifestyle. Veye will give you your protein requirement and help you maintain lean muscle mass.

### Is there protein in cantaloupe, broccoli?

Although there may be trace amounts, there is not enough to activate glucagon or AMPK, and therefore these are not good protein sources.
