# Health Number guide

A plain-language explanation of the Veye Health Number for members. Synthetic demo knowledge written from the approved product wording; it contains no member information.

## What the Health Number is

Your Health Number comes from the twelve questions you answer when you start with Veye: your goals, any eating plan you follow, how active you are, whether you meditate, how tired you feel, weight and abdominal weight, and three questions about sleep. It is shown on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is very healthy and 10 is very unhealthy. A lower Health Number is better.

## How the scale reads

A Health Number of 1 means good health: you are in very good health, and Veye helps you learn about optimizing your health, preventing disease and slowing the aging process.

A Health Number above 1 and up to 3.5 means relatively good health. Some lifestyle changes, some changes to your dietary program, or a mix of both will help you optimize your health, prevent disease and slow the aging process.

A Health Number above 3.5 and up to 6 means moderately good health. Incorporating better food choices and implementing some lifestyle changes will help you optimize your health, prevent disease and slow the aging process.

A Health Number above 6 shows signs of insulin resistance, which can lead to chronic disease or a worsening of established chronic disease. The Veye program helps you make changes so you feel better and live longer.

## Lifestyle, food or mixed

Veye also looks at whether the answers that raised your number were mostly about lifestyle (activity, meditation, tiredness, sleep) or mostly about food (eating plans, weight, abdominal weight). The interpretation you read on your dashboard reflects that: it points to lifestyle changes, to your dietary program, or to both when the two are tied.

## Re-taking the assessment

You can re-take the Health Number assessment whenever you like from My Progress. Every result is kept, so the history shows how your number has moved over time. The most recent result is the one shown on your dashboard.

## What the Health Number is not

The Health Number is not a diagnosis and it does not replace advice from your doctor. Body composition and blood test markers are separate trackers in My Progress; they do not change the Health Number.
